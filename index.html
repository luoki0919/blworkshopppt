THE DEFINITION & FLOW
What is Scenario-Based Learning?
Learning happens inside a realistic situation — not only inside an explanation.
Core Elements
Seven Key SBL Ingredients
🎬 Realistic Scenarios 👤 Characters 🏫 Context ⏱️ Pressure ⚖️ Choices 🌊 Consequences 🪞 Reflection
  • Combines situated learning theory with authentic storytelling.
  • Puts learners in realistic roles with proper emotional stakes.
Pedagogical Value
Why Branching Specifically?
  • Every decision has consequences: Learners see immediate ripple effects.
  • Every choice reveals new information: Drives active exploration.
  • Mistakes are safe: Allows failing safely without real damage.
  • Unique paths: Comparison between paths is where social reflection occurs.

INSTRUCTIONAL DESIGN
Crafting Decision Points: Good, Better & Best
The 3C Model: Challenge → Choice → Consequence. Four choices × four choices = up to 16 paths.
1. The Hook
🎯 Challenge
An authentic dilemma with uncertain outcomes. Rooted in student reality: integrity under pressure, academic stress, interpersonal friction.
2. The Dilemma
⚖️ Choice
3 to 4 viable paths. No obvious "throwaway" answers. Each represents a believable reaction an actual university student might make.
3. The Ripple
🌊 Consequence
Immediate feedback or delayed impacts. Reveals how small choices early on cause large ripples downstream.
Design Rules
💸 Optimal Choice & Cost
  • One choice per branch is the more optimal choice.
  • Even the optimal choice should still cost something.
  • Keep all option descriptions approximately equal in length.

Design Tip: You don't have to write 16 totally different stories. Let paths converge, but carry the consequence state forward.
The Competition Rule in One Line: If one option is obviously right, the player learns nothing. Ambiguity creates the learning tension.

SUBMISSION SKELETON
Every Compliant Entry Has This Structure
Fail any of these hard checks and your entry is rejected as incomplete.
🎬 Two Decisions & Branching Structure
1. Intro Clip (<1 min): Sets scene, character, and central tension.
↓
2. Decision 1 (3–4 choices): Genuine dilemma with trade-offs.
↓
3. Branch Clips & Decision 2 (3–4 choices): Deeper consequential crisis.
↓
4. Terminal Outcomes & Debriefs: Every ending mapped to framework.
📋 Checklist That Decides Pass / Fail
  • ✓ Two decision levels: Exactly two; never binary (yes/no).
  • ✓ English only: All script, debriefs, and video subtitles.
  • ✓ Short clips rule: Each clip under 1 min in MP4 format (PPT animation export allowed).
  • ✓ GenAI declaration: Mandatory disclosure (no penalty for using AI, but undeclared breaks rules).
  • ✓ Open downloadable folder: Link accessible without login.
  • ✓ Team limit: ≤ 4 students (individual entries permitted).
The Video Bottleneck: Roughly 8–10 short clips are required in total. This is why 70% of student teams stall or drop out — and exactly why we built the automated pipeline.
